## 📌 About the Project

The **Sumit Steel Website** is a business-oriented web project developed to present the company's products and services through a professional online interface.

Sumit Steel specializes in iron and steel products, profile cutting, dealership, and stockist services. The website communicates the business's focus on product quality, reliable supply, timely delivery, and long-term customer relationships.

### 🎯 Project Objectives

- Establish a professional online presence for Sumit Steel.
- Showcase available iron and steel products.
- Present profile cutting and related business services.
- Make contact information and business location accessible.
- Allow prospective customers to submit product enquiries and quotation requests.
- Improve the accessibility of business information for potential customers.

## ✨ Website Features

### 🏠 1. Home Page
- Introduces Sumit Steel and its business offerings.
- Highlights its iron and steel products and services.
- Provides quick access to product information and quotation requests.

### 🏢 2. About Us
- Presents the business and its experience in the steel industry.
- Communicates its emphasis on quality, reliability, and customer relationships.

### 🧱 3. Product Catalogue
The website showcases a range of iron and steel products, including:

- I-Beams
- Steel Channels
- MS Angles
- Steel Flats
- Round Bars
- Mild Steel (MS) Plates
- Hot-Rolled (HR) Sheets
- Cold-Rolled (CR) Sheets
- Chequered Plates
- Other steel products available on request

Customers can use the enquiry links to ask about specific products and requirements.

### ⚙️ 4. Profile Cutting Services
The website presents a customer-oriented process for profile cutting:

1. Share the cutting requirements.
2. Select the required material.
3. Carry out precision cutting.
4. Prepare the order for delivery.
